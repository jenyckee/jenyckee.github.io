Training for 20 km with Garmin MCP, OpenCode, and OpenRouter

I’m training for a 20 km run, and part of my setup is a tool I normally associate with writing code: OpenCode. The idea is simple. My Garmin records my runs and recovery data. An MCP server makes that information available to the agent. OpenRouter provides access to the language model.
