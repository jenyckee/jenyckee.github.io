Visualising Strava bike rides [draft]

In the mourning process about last summer I decided to build something nice. Like most of us I meticulously record every bike ride I do with a popular app among athletes called Strava. Strava is often described as the Facebook for athletes. It works by simply hitting the start button when going out for a run or bike ride and it will record a series of GPS coordinates until you hit stop again. Afterwards the athlete gets a report that shows a map with the route followed alongside with some graphs on speed, hearth rate and other data. [read more]
